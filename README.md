# 📚 Social Media Usage and Academic Performance Analysis

## Overview

This repository contains the research project **"The Impact of Social Media Usage on Academic Performance and Productivity Behaviours Among Students."** The study investigates how different patterns of social media usage influence students' academic performance, productivity, and study habits using survey-based statistical analysis.

---

## Objectives

- Examine the relationship between social media usage and academic performance.
- Analyze behavioural factors such as procrastination, distraction, and study habits.
- Evaluate whether social media usage during study sessions has a significant impact on academic outcomes.

---

## Methodology

- **Research Design:** Survey-based quantitative study
- **Sample Size:** 50 university students
- **Data Collection:** Google Forms questionnaire
- **Study Type:** Academic Research Project

---

## Statistical Methods

The study employed the following statistical techniques:

- Spearman Rank Correlation
- Mann–Whitney U Test
- Kruskal–Wallis Test
- Ordinary Least Squares (OLS) Regression
- Descriptive Statistical Analysis

## Key Findings

- Social media usage during study sessions showed a stronger relationship with reduced academic productivity than overall daily screen time.
- Behavioural indicators such as distraction and procrastination were associated with lower academic performance.
- Statistical analysis provided insights into factors affecting students' study efficiency.
